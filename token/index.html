Token · draft

The night shift's token.

$ONYRA is the token designed for Onyra Cloud: to pay for credits and compute, to stake for priority, and to carry a vote when governance exists. It has not launched.

Not an offer Nothing on this page is an offer to sell a token, a solicitation, or a promise of profit or of supply. The percentages below are a draft for discussion.

01 — Utility

Three uses. None of them are active.

Not active

Cloud credits

Pay for Onyra Cloud credits and the compute a task consumes. This is the use the token is being written around.

Not active

Priority

Stake to move ahead in the queue, once staking exists. No rate is stated, because inventing one would be a promise.

Not active

Governance

Vote on treasury and on parameters, once governance exists. Until that mechanism is published, there is nothing to vote on.

02 — Tokenomics

A table with a stamp on it.

Draft

Illustrative shares, so the shape of the design can be read. They are not a cap table, a final supply, or a commitment. The real allocation will be published with the contract, not before.

Draft allocation — for discussion only
Bucket Draft share What it is for
Cloud credit reserve 35% Compute and hosting credits over time.
Community 25% Programs, to be specified before any launch.
Treasury 15% Held for governance, once governance exists.
Core contributors 15% The people building Onyra, with a lockup published alongside the contract.
Liquidity 10% Pools at launch, if a public market is opened.

03 — Contract

One address, one file.

When a contract exists, its address is entered once, in js/config.js. The chip in the footer, the home page, and this panel all read that same value. Today the value is empty, so every page says the same thing.

Do not trust an address sent in a reply, a direct message, or a lookalike site. If it is not the address this page shows, it is not ours.
